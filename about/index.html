About me

Hi, I'm Elise.

This Little Soirée grew out of something I've always loved: bringing people together.

My story

I found my way into events naturally, by hosting get-togethers for friends. I've always been the one picking out the décor, hunting down the perfect pieces to pull a theme together, and heading to the flower market to build the bouquets myself. Those details are what make guests feel looked after, and they're what I love most.

That passion grew into a business, and the business led me to my role as contract Chief of Staff for YEARN, an international series of business seminars and music festivals.

Growing with YEARN

Since 2021, I've watched YEARN grow from a few friends in California coming together to talk business and dance all night into international events across New York, Los Angeles, San Francisco, Desert Hot Springs and Playa del Carmen. Each event pairs seminars led by Silicon Valley CEOs with a full music festival featuring DJs from around the world. Our most recent festival welcomed almost 400 guests.

Whether it's 20 guests around a candlelit table or 400 on a dance floor, every guest should feel personally welcomed.

How I work

What makes my work special is that it has stayed personal as it has grown. I'm the direct contact for every guest, vendor and staff member, and every email and invitation comes from me.

I'm involved in every stage, from shaping the first ideas to standing on site with a hammer in hand, building the setup myself. Nothing gets handed off and forgotten.

I've returned to the same resort partner in Playa del Carmen time and again, for both YEARN and Nativo. I build relationships with venues and vendors that last beyond a single event.
